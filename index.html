What is spatial intelligence?

The ability of an AI system to construct, maintain and manipulate spatial representations of the physical world that support perception, reasoning, prediction and embodied action.

The full definition, Section 2.2 →

Five eras of representing space

Each era is defined by what it took space to be, and each left a problem for the next.
