What is Spatial Intelligence?

Spatial intelligence is the ability to perceive, reason about, and generate three-dimensional worlds. This book provides the first comprehensive treatment of spatial intelligence in AI, covering everything from foundational representations to real-world applications.

Key Features

Six qualities that shape how this survey is written, organized, and used

01

Comprehensive Coverage

The survey spans the full arc of spatial intelligence, from classical geometry and camera models to the modern foundation-model era. Representations are treated as one connected family rather than separate silos: 2D image-level understanding, 3D scene-level reconstruction, and 4D space-time modeling are each given their own chapter while sharing a common vocabulary for pose, depth, and structure. Perception, reasoning, and generation are covered in equal depth, so the reader can follow a single thread from pixels to physically grounded action.

  • 2D / 3D / 4D representations
  • Perception · Reasoning · Generation
  • Classical geometry → foundation models
02

Cutting-Edge Research

Every chapter is written against the state of the art through 2026, not a historical summary. The text walks through the methods that reshaped the field in recent years — Neural Radiance Fields, 3D Gaussian Splatting, feed-forward reconstruction, and Vision-Language-Action models — alongside the world models and spatial reasoning benchmarks that define where the field is heading next. Recent work is not just cited but placed in context, with the assumptions and limitations of each line of research made explicit.

  • NeRF & 3D Gaussian Splatting
  • Vision-Language-Action models
  • Literature current through 2026
03

Real-World Applications

Spatial intelligence only matters when it reaches the physical world. A dedicated part of the book traces how the same core capabilities are applied across embodied AI and robotics, extended reality, autonomous driving, geospatial analysis, healthcare, and scientific discovery. Each application chapter starts from the domain's requirements — latency, safety, accuracy — and works backward to the representations and models that satisfy them, so the connection between a method and its deployment is never left implicit.

  • Embodied AI · XR · autonomous driving
  • Geospatial · healthcare · science
  • Requirements-driven domain chapters
04

Systematic Organization

The book follows a deliberate progression: foundations, then representations, then core capabilities, then applications, and finally open problems. Each of the 17 chapters closes with benchmarks, available tools, and unsolved challenges, so readers leave with an actionable map of the subfield rather than a list of papers. Cross-references between chapters make it possible to read the book linearly or to enter at any point that matches the reader's background.

  • 5 parts · 17 chapters
  • Benchmarks & tools in every chapter
  • Cross-referenced, modular structure
05

Academic Rigor

Over 2,500 references have been curated rather than collected: each is tied to a specific claim, method, or evaluation protocol. Methods are compared on consistent criteria, datasets and metrics are introduced with their known biases, and evaluation protocols are described precisely enough to be reproduced. Where the literature disagrees, the survey presents the competing positions instead of flattening them into a single conclusion.

  • 2,500+ curated references
  • Consistent method comparisons
  • Reproducible evaluation protocols
06

Educational Resource

The book is written to be taught from as well as cited. Chapters are largely self-contained, key concepts are introduced before they are used, and recurring notation is kept consistent across parts. Researchers can use it as a reference for the current state of the field, students can read it as a structured course from foundations to applications, and practitioners can jump to the application chapter closest to their deployment problem.

  • Self-contained chapters
  • Consistent notation throughout
  • For researchers, students & practitioners
